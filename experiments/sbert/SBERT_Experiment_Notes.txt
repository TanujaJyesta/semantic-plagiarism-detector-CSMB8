# SBERT Experiment – Preliminary Results

**Objective:** To evaluate semantic similarity between different sentence pairs using SBERT.

**Model:** sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2

**Implementation:** Python, Sentence Transformers and cosine similarity.

**Results:**

| Pair Type | SBERT Score |
|---|---:|
| Exact match | 1.0000 |
| Paraphrased | 0.8341 |
| Reworded | 0.8033 |
| Unrelated | 0.0919 |
| Hard negative | 0.7248 |

**Observations:**
1. SBERT achieved a high score for paraphrased and reworded sentences.
2. It assigned a low score to unrelated sentences.
3. Contradictory sentences still received a relatively high similarity score.

**Conclusion:** SBERT captures semantic relationships better than TF-IDF in these preliminary examples. However, semantic similarity alone cannot reliably distinguish contradictory statements.

**Status:** Preliminary experiment completed. Further evaluation on a larger dataset is required.
